# Roadmap

This roadmap is the master plan for the curriculum. Each stage has a purpose, a sequence of study units, and a capstone or milestone deliverable.

## Stage Sequence

1. `stage-00-machine`
2. `stage-01-c`
3. `stage-02-cpp`
4. `stage-03-systems`
5. `stage-04-holyc`
6. `stage-05-csharp`
7. `stage-06-python`
8. `stage-07-sql`
9. `stage-08-typescript`
10. `stage-09-rust`

## Notes

- Do not rush through a stage.
- Revisit concepts until they are intuitive.
- Each stage should end with evidence: code, notes, and/or a report.
- Portfolio-level work belongs outside this repo.

## Milestone Questions

For each stage, answer:

- What is the core mental model?
- What primitives or language features matter?
- What problems does this stage solve well?
- What mistakes have I made while learning?
- What project-level evidence can I produce from this knowledge?

## Progress Expectations

- Weekly check-ins
- Unit completion tracking
- Capstone reflection
- Report-card review
